Every claim, labeled.

Most peptide content on the internet treats every number as fact. We don't. Every claim on every page carries one of these four labels — so you know whether a half-life is from a Phase-III trial or a Reddit thread before you act on it.

well-established

Replicated trials with n > 100, mature peer-reviewed evidence, FDA precedent.

emerging

Small or single trials, animal-to-human extrapolation, early Phase 2 data.

community

User reports, dated forum threads, community surveys — cited and verifiable, not invented.

unknown

No data exists. We say so plainly rather than fill space.
