Most nootropic peptides have a Russian clinical research base, dating from Soviet-era pharmaceutical development. Semax and Selank are intranasal heptapeptides approved in Russia for cognitive enhancement and anxiety. Cerebrolysin is a porcine-brain-derived peptide complex approved in 50+ countries for stroke and dementia. Dihexa and P21 are American academic spinouts targeting Alzheimer pathways.

Oxytocin is the most-studied of the cognitive peptides — investigated for social anxiety, autism, and PTSD with mixed but interesting data. DSIP, P21, and Pinealon remain firmly in the research-chemical category with sparse human evidence.

Frequently asked

Common questions.

How does Semax compare to Selank?

Semax targets BDNF/NGF upregulation — useful for focus, motivation, and recovery from stroke. Selank targets GABA/serotonin modulation — useful for anxiety without sedation. They are sometimes stacked.

Is Cerebrolysin safe?

Cerebrolysin has decades of clinical use in Europe, Russia, and Asia. The most rigorous meta-analyses show modest but consistent benefits in stroke recovery; the dementia data is more variable.

Does intranasal oxytocin actually work?

Intranasal oxytocin shows acute effects on trust, social cognition, and stress reactivity. The clinical-trial literature in autism and PTSD is mixed — promising acute studies, less impressive long-term outcomes.
