01 · Quick Overview

Quick Overview.

Semax is a synthetic heptapeptide (a chain of seven amino acids) developed in Russia in the 1980s. It is an analog of a fragment of adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH), specifically ACTH(4-10), but modified with the addition of a Pro-Gly-Pro sequence to protect it from rapid degradation in the body. In Russia and Eastern Europe, it is an approved medical drug used extensively to treat stroke, traumatic brain injury, and cognitive decline.[1]

In the biohacking and nootropic communities, Semax is highly prized for its profound ability to enhance focus, memory, and mental clarity without the jittery side effects of traditional stimulants like Adderall or caffeine. It is administered intranasally (via nasal spray) or subcutaneously, allowing it to rapidly cross the blood-brain barrier.[2]

  • Primary Use Case: Cognitive enhancement, ADHD management, stroke recovery, and neuroprotection.
  • Mechanism: Massive upregulation of Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) and Nerve Growth Factor (NGF), alongside modulation of dopamine and serotonin receptors.[3]
  • Who it is for: Students, executives, and biohackers looking for a clean, non-stimulant cognitive boost, or individuals recovering from brain injuries.
  • Who it is NOT for: Individuals looking for a physical performance enhancer (it has no direct effect on muscle growth).

02 · The Protocol & Usage Guide

The Protocol & Usage Guide.

confidence_tier: well-established

Semax is most commonly administered via an intranasal spray. Because peptides are fragile, the nasal spray must be kept refrigerated. There are two primary versions available on the market: standard Semax and N-Acetyl Semax Amidate (NASA), which has been modified for better stability and blood-brain barrier penetration.[4]

Cycle Length & Discontinuation Protocol

  • Cycle Length: Typically used on an "as-needed" basis (e.g., before a big exam or presentation) or in cycles of 4 to 6 weeks, followed by 2 to 4 weeks off to prevent receptor downregulation.
  • Discontinuation: Semax does not cause physical dependence or withdrawal symptoms. It can be stopped abruptly without a tapering protocol.

07 · Storage, Handling & Accessibility

Storage, Handling & Accessibility.

confidence_tier: well-established

  • Storage: Must be refrigerated. Semax is a fragile peptide. If left at room temperature for more than a few days, it will degrade and lose potency.
  • WADA Status: Semax is banned by WADA in-competition under section S2 (Peptide Hormones, Growth Factors, Related Substances, and Mimetics).
  • Cost & Accessibility: Widely available through nootropic and peptide vendors online. In Russia, it is available over-the-counter at pharmacies.
08 · Bloodwork Monitoring Guide

Bloodwork Monitoring Guide.

10 · Deep Dive (For Advanced Researchers)

Deep Dive (For Advanced Researchers).

confidence_tier: well-established

The BDNF and NGF Upregulation Mechanism

Semax's most profound physiological effect is its ability to rapidly and massively upregulate the expression of neurotrophins.

  • In animal models, a single intranasal dose of Semax has been shown to increase Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) expression in the hippocampus and frontal cortex by up to 800% within 24 hours.
  • It simultaneously increases Nerve Growth Factor (NGF) expression by up to 500%.
  • These neurotrophins are critical for neuroplasticity—the brain's ability to form new synaptic connections. This explains why Semax is so effective for learning, memory consolidation, and recovery from ischemic strokes.[5][6]

The Pro-Gly-Pro Modification

The base sequence of Semax is ACTH(4-10), a naturally occurring peptide fragment that has known cognitive benefits but a half-life of only a few minutes in the blood. Russian scientists attached a Proline-Glycine-Proline (Pro-Gly-Pro) sequence to the C-terminus of the peptide. This specific tripeptide sequence is highly resistant to enzymatic degradation by peptidases in the blood and brain. This modification extends the half-life of Semax to several hours and allows it to survive the journey across the nasal mucosa and the blood-brain barrier.[7]

Neuroprotection in Ischemic Stroke

In Russia, Semax 1% solution is a standard-of-care treatment for acute ischemic stroke.

  • Clinical trials have demonstrated that administering Semax within the first few hours of a stroke significantly reduces the size of the infarct (the area of dead brain tissue).
  • It achieves this by inhibiting nitric oxide synthesis, reducing glutamate excitotoxicity, and suppressing the expression of pro-inflammatory cytokines in the brain. It essentially puts the brain into a "survival mode" while simultaneously triggering the growth of new neurons via BDNF.[8][9]
11 · Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ).

confidence_tier: community

Q: Will Semax make me fail a drug test? A: Yes, if you are a WADA-tested athlete. It is banned in-competition. However, for standard workplace drug tests (which look for amphetamines, THC, opiates, etc.), Semax will not trigger a false positive.

Q: Why does my Semax burn when I spray it? A: Standard Semax should not burn. However, N-Acetyl Semax Amidate (NASA) is often suspended in a slightly acidic solution to maintain stability, which can cause a brief, mild stinging sensation in the nasal cavity.

Q: Can I take Semax every day forever? A: It is not recommended. While it is very safe, constantly forcing the brain to upregulate BDNF may eventually lead to TrkB receptor downregulation (the receptor that BDNF binds to). Cycling (e.g., 5 days on, 2 days off) is best practice.
