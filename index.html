Protocols

Every vial. Every dose. Every cycle. Tracked.

Add a vial in 30 seconds. Epti handles the reconstitution math, calculates units to draw per dose, tracks remaining volume, warns before you run out, and logs every injection with site rotation and reminders.

  • 01Reconstitution and dose math, done for you. Enter the vial, the BAC water, and the target dose; Epti tells you exactly how many units to draw and shows it on a labeled syringe so there's no mental math at 6am.
  • 02Reminders that know your protocol phase. Pre-dose, due, late, and skipped states with surface alerts that respect timing windows — not just calendar pings that fire whether you're loading, cruising, or cycling off.
  • 03Site rotation that prevents scar tissue and lipohypertrophy. An interactive body map tracks every injection by site, depth, and angle, then routes the next dose to the spot that's had the longest recovery.
  • 04Side effects linked to the dose that caused them. Log symptoms by severity and Epti correlates them with compound, dose, timing, and stack — so you can see whether the headache is the Tirzepatide or just your week.
  • 05A protocol history that actually compounds. Active, paused, and archived cycles in one view, with cost-per-cycle, total volume injected, and outcome notes — so cycle four learns from cycles one through three.

The engine
Adaptive engine

A program that rewrites itself around you.

Every morning, Epti reads last night's recovery, your active stack, and what's on the board — then writes a Daily Brief in plain English explaining exactly what's changing today and why. Tap to accept the adjustments, or skip what doesn't fit.

  • 01Adjustments you can accept, skip, or modify. Volume, calories, water, and bedtime suggestions arrive as recommendations, not prescriptions — one tap to apply, one tap to ignore, never imposed without your sign-off.
  • 02Macros and training load that match your phase. Whether you're in a fat-loss block, recomp, gaining phase, or maintenance, Epti adjusts protein floor, calorie target, and training volume to match what you're actually trying to do this cycle.
  • 03Strength tracked against your protocol. Every PR plotted against the cycle that produced it, alongside sleep, bodyweight, and HRV — so you can see exactly which protocols built strength and which just made the scale move.
  • 04Drift detection before you waste a cycle. If your weight, strength, or biomarkers stop responding the way your protocol predicts, Epti flags the divergence early — so you can adjust mid-cycle instead of finding out at week 12 that nothing worked.

Nutrition

Snap it. Scan it. Speak it. Logged.

Four ways to capture a meal, none of them painful. Photograph the plate, scan a barcode, describe what you ate in plain English, or tap a saved favorite. Epti reads the calories and macros, then nets them against today's training burn for a clean running delta.

  • 01Logging that doesn't get in the way of eating. Snap a photo, scan a barcode, type what you ate, or speak it — Epti picks the method that works for the meal and gets out of the way.
  • 02Restaurant and packaged food, solved. Barcode and restaurant QR scanning for chain meals and packaged food, with macros that actually match the label.
  • 03Built for what we actually eat. Small meals, partial portions, and "I only had half of it" — text and voice logging work better than photo recognition when the meal is two eggs and half a piece of toast.
  • 04One-tap repeats for the meals you eat every day. Save your three breakfasts, four lunches, and rotation of dinners — log a full day in under 30 seconds once your patterns are in.
  • 05Calorie targets that respect your peptide phase. Fuel in minus training out, with protein floors and calorie targets adjusted for your phase — so you don't accidentally under-eat through a loading week and lose the muscle you're trying to keep.
