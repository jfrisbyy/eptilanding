The anabolic-vs-suppression tradeoff

There is no free lunch with SARMs. The compounds that produce the strongest anabolic effect (RAD-140, LGD-4033) also produce the heaviest HPG suppression. Ostarine, the mildest, also has the weakest anabolic effect. Pick based on what side of that tradeoff matters more to you.

When to pick Ostarine

Recomp cycles, bridge cycles between AAS cycles, injury recovery, first-time SARM users. The most studied SARM (FDA Phase III for cancer cachexia), the mildest suppression, and the lowest reported side-effect rate. Often run without PCT at 15 mg / day, though SERM PCT is still advisable.

When to pick LGD-4033

Lean strength, recomp with more aggression than Ostarine can deliver, clean dryness profile. Suppresses HPG hard — needs a full 4-week PCT with Nolvadex or Enclomiphene. Sourcing risk: heavily counterfeited.

When to pick RAD-140

Maximum anabolic effect SARMs can offer, near-testosterone levels of muscle and strength gains. The hepatotoxicity case reports (a handful of published liver-injury cases) and aggressive HPG suppression mean this is the SARM with the highest risk profile. Full PCT mandatory.
