01 · Quick Overview

Quick Overview.

Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide (a chain of seven amino acids) developed by the Institute of Molecular Genetics of the Russian Academy of Sciences. It is a stabilized analog of Tuftsin, a naturally occurring peptide produced in the spleen that plays a role in immune function. In Russia, Selank is an approved medical drug used primarily as an anxiolytic (anti-anxiety medication) and a mild nootropic.[1]

In the biohacking community, Selank is highly regarded as a "clean" alternative to benzodiazepines (like Xanax or Valium). It provides profound anxiety relief, emotional stability, and mental clarity without causing sedation, cognitive impairment, or physical dependence. Like its cousin Semax, it is administered intranasally to bypass the digestive system and rapidly cross the blood-brain barrier.[2]

  • Primary Use Case: Generalized anxiety disorder (GAD), social anxiety, stress management, and mild cognitive enhancement.
  • Mechanism: Modulation of GABAergic neurotransmission, stabilization of enkephalin degradation, and upregulation of Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF).[3]
  • Who it is for: Individuals suffering from acute or chronic anxiety, high-stress professionals, and those looking to "smooth out" the jittery effects of stimulants.
  • Who it is NOT for: Individuals looking for a strong sedative or sleep aid (Selank calms the mind but does not induce sleep).

02 · The Protocol & Usage Guide

The Protocol & Usage Guide.

confidence_tier: well-established

Selank is most commonly administered via an intranasal spray. Because it is a fragile peptide, the nasal spray must be kept refrigerated. There are two primary versions available on the market: standard Selank and N-Acetyl Selank Amidate (NASA), which has been modified for better stability and blood-brain barrier penetration.[4]

Standard Dosing Schedule

PhaseDoseFrequencyRoute
Standard Anxiety Relief200 mcg to 400 mcg1-3x dailyIntranasal
Acute Panic / High Stress400 mcg to 600 mcgAs neededIntranasal
N-Acetyl Selank Amidate100 mcg to 300 mcg1-2x dailyIntranasal

Cycle Length & Discontinuation Protocol

  • Cycle Length: Can be used on an "as-needed" basis for acute stress, or in cycles of 2 to 4 weeks for generalized anxiety.
  • Discontinuation: Unlike benzodiazepines, Selank does not cause physical dependence, tolerance, or withdrawal symptoms. It can be stopped abruptly without a tapering protocol.

04 · Safety, Interactions & Side Effect Management

Safety, Interactions & Side Effect Management.

confidence_tier: well-established

Side Effect Profile

Side EffectSeverityFrequencyManagement
LethargyMildRareOccurs if the dose is pushed too high. Reduce the dose.
HeadacheMildRareUsually related to the nasal spray vehicle or mild dehydration.
Hair LossMildVery RareBDNF upregulation can theoretically accelerate hair loss in genetically prone individuals, though less common than with Semax.

Contraindications

  • Absolute: None strictly defined in clinical literature, though it should not be used by pregnant or nursing women due to a lack of safety data.
  • Relative: Individuals currently taking high doses of prescription benzodiazepines or barbiturates should consult a physician, as Selank modulates the same GABAergic pathways and may alter the effects of these drugs.

  • Pre-Workout Anxiety: Many athletes experience "performance anxiety" before a heavy lift or a competition. Selank is highly favored in these scenarios because it removes the anxiety without acting as a muscle relaxant or sedative (which would ruin power output).

07 · Storage, Handling & Accessibility

Storage, Handling & Accessibility.

confidence_tier: well-established

  • Storage: Must be refrigerated. Selank is a fragile peptide. If left at room temperature for more than a few days, it will degrade and lose potency.
  • WADA Status: Selank is not explicitly listed on the WADA prohibited list, but athletes should exercise extreme caution, as WADA has a blanket ban on "non-approved" peptide hormones.
  • Cost & Accessibility: Widely available through nootropic and peptide vendors online. In Russia, it is available over-the-counter at pharmacies.

10 · Deep Dive (For Advanced Researchers)

Deep Dive (For Advanced Researchers).

confidence_tier: well-established

GABAergic Modulation Without Sedation

The most remarkable pharmacological trait of Selank is its ability to act as an anxiolytic without causing the sedation, amnesia, or motor impairment associated with traditional GABAergic drugs (like benzodiazepines or alcohol).

  • Allosteric Modulation: Clinical studies show that Selank binds to specific allosteric sites on the GABA-A receptor. However, unlike benzodiazepines, which forcefully keep the ion channel open, Selank appears to act as a modulator that normalizes GABAergic tone. It enhances GABA transmission when the brain is overexcited, but does not depress the central nervous system below baseline.[5][6]
  • Gene Expression: Research on human neuroblastoma cells has demonstrated that Selank rapidly alters the expression of over 30 genes involved in GABAergic neurotransmission, suggesting a profound regulatory effect at the genomic level.[7]

Enkephalin Stabilization

Selank has been shown to inhibit the enzymes that degrade enkephalins (naturally occurring opioid peptides in the brain). By preventing the breakdown of these endogenous "feel-good" peptides, Selank increases their concentration in the brain, contributing to its mood-stabilizing and mild antidepressant effects.[8]

BDNF Upregulation

Like Semax, Selank has been shown to upregulate Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) in the hippocampus.

  • In animal models, intranasal administration of Selank significantly increased BDNF mRNA expression within 24 hours.
  • This neurotrophic effect explains why Selank is considered a nootropic; by promoting neuroplasticity, it helps the brain "unlearn" chronic anxiety pathways and form healthier synaptic connections.[9]
11 · Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ).

confidence_tier: community

Q: Will Selank help me sleep? A: Selank is not a sedative. If your insomnia is caused by racing thoughts and anxiety, Selank will help calm your mind so you can fall asleep naturally. However, it will not "knock you out" like a sleeping pill.

Q: Why does my Selank burn when I spray it? A: Standard Selank should not burn. However, N-Acetyl Selank Amidate (NASA) is often suspended in a slightly acidic solution to maintain stability, which can cause a brief, mild stinging sensation in the nasal cavity.

Q: Can I drink alcohol while taking Selank? A: While there are no severe, life-threatening interactions reported, alcohol and Selank both act on the GABA system. Selank has actually been shown in Russian studies to reduce the intoxicating and depressive effects of alcohol, but combining them is generally not recommended.
