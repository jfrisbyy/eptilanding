This category covers compounds that are either pre-clinical, FDA-approved for narrow indications and used off-label, or sold as supplements. Evidence quality ranges from robust (PT-141 for HSDD) to thin (Epitalon, NNMT inhibitors) to actively concerning (Melanotan II melanoma data).

We label every claim by confidence — well-established, emerging, community, or unknown. Read with the gradient in mind.

Frequently asked

Common questions.

What does "research peptide" actually mean?

Practically, it means the compound is sold without FDA approval for human use, often labeled "not for human consumption." Quality, dosing, and purity vary widely between suppliers, and third-party testing reveals frequent under-dosing and contamination.

Which research peptides have the best human data?

PT-141 (bremelanotide) is FDA-approved for HSDD; Melanotan I (afamelanotide / Scenesse) is FDA-approved for EPP. Most others have only small pilot or animal data.
