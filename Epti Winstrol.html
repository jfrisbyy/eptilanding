01 · Quick Overview

Quick Overview.

Winstrol (chemical name: Stanozolol) is one of the most famous and widely used anabolic-androgenic steroids (AAS) in the world. It is a derivative of Dihydrotestosterone (DHT) and is available in both oral (tablet) and injectable (water-based suspension) forms. It gained global infamy in 1988 when Olympic sprinter Ben Johnson was stripped of his gold medal after testing positive for it.[1]

In the bodybuilding and athletic communities, Winstrol is prized for its ability to produce extreme muscle hardness, vascularity, and explosive strength without any water retention. Because it cannot aromatize into estrogen, it creates a very "dry" look. However, it is notoriously harsh on the body—it is highly liver-toxic, destroys lipid profiles, and frequently causes severe joint pain by drying out the synovial fluid.[2]

  • Primary Use Case: Pre-contest hardening, explosive strength for athletes, and cutting.
  • Mechanism: Agonism of the androgen receptor and profound reduction of Sex Hormone-Binding Globulin (SHBG).[3]
  • Who it is for: Advanced bodybuilders in the final 4-6 weeks of contest prep, or strength athletes seeking power without weight gain.
  • Who it is NOT for: Beginners, anyone with pre-existing joint issues, or anyone concerned about their liver and cardiovascular health.

02 · The Protocol & Usage Guide

The Protocol & Usage Guide.

confidence_tier: well-established

Winstrol is unique because both the oral and injectable versions are 17-alpha-alkylated (17aa). This means the injectable version is just as liver-toxic as the oral version. Most users prefer the oral version to avoid the extreme pain associated with injecting the water-based suspension.[4]

Standard Dosing Schedule

PhaseDoseFrequencyForm
Men (Standard Cutting)30 mg to 50 mg / daySplit into 2 dosesOral
Men (Advanced Pre-Contest)50 mg to 100 mg / daySplit into 2 dosesOral or Injectable
Women (Beginner)5 mg / daySingle doseOral
Women (Advanced)10 mg to 15 mg / daySplit into 2 dosesOral

Cycle Length & Discontinuation Protocol

  • Cycle Length: Strictly 4 to 6 weeks. Running Winstrol longer than 6 weeks guarantees severe liver stress and lipid destruction.
  • The "Test Base" Rule: Winstrol suppresses natural testosterone. Men must run it alongside a Testosterone base to prevent lethargy and sexual dysfunction.
  • Discontinuation (PCT): A standard PCT (Nolvadex/Clomid) is required for men if not cruising on TRT.

06 · Body Composition & Training Guide

Body Composition & Training Guide.

confidence_tier: well-established

  • The "Winstrol Look": Winstrol does not build massive amounts of tissue. Instead, it acts as a cosmetic hardener. If a user is under 10% body fat, Winstrol will make their skin look paper-thin and their muscles look like granite.
  • Explosive Strength: It is highly favored by sprinters, fighters, and athletes because it dramatically increases power output and speed without adding any water weight that would slow the athlete down.
  • The Joint Paradox: While it makes you stronger, it dries out your joints. Many bodybuilders tear muscles or ligaments while on Winstrol because their muscles are strong enough to lift the weight, but their dry joints and tendons cannot support the load.
07 · Storage, Handling & Accessibility

Storage, Handling & Accessibility.

confidence_tier: well-established

  • Storage: Store tablets at room temperature. If using the injectable suspension, shake vigorously before use, as the hormone separates from the water.
  • WADA Status: Banned in competitive sports under section S1.1 (Anabolic Androgenic Steroids). It is highly detectable; metabolites can be found in urine for months after the last dose.
  • Cost & Accessibility: Widely available and very inexpensive to produce. It is one of the most common oral steroids on the black market.

10 · Deep Dive (For Advanced Researchers)

Deep Dive (For Advanced Researchers).

confidence_tier: well-established

The SHBG Reduction Mechanism

One of Winstrol's most unique and powerful pharmacological traits is its profound ability to lower Sex Hormone-Binding Globulin (SHBG). SHBG is a protein produced by the liver that binds to sex hormones (like testosterone) in the blood, rendering them temporarily inactive.

  • Clinical studies have shown that a dose of just 0.2 mg/kg of Stanozolol can lower SHBG levels by up to 50% within a few days.
  • By lowering SHBG, Winstrol frees up the accompanying testosterone in the user's cycle. This means that adding Winstrol to a testosterone cycle makes the testosterone significantly more effective, as more of it exists in a "free," unbound state.[5]

Hepatotoxicity and the 17-Alpha-Alkylation

Stanozolol is 17-alpha-alkylated, which prevents its destruction by the liver. However, this structural change places immense stress on hepatic function.

  • A distinctive clinical phenotype of Stanozolol-induced liver injury is severe cholestasis (impaired bile flow). Users frequently present with highly elevated bilirubin, jaundice (yellowing of the skin and eyes), and severe pruritus (itching).
  • Unlike Anavar, which is partially excreted unchanged in the urine, Stanozolol relies heavily on hepatic metabolism, making it significantly more toxic.[6][7]

The Joint Pain Phenomenon

The exact mechanism behind "Winstrol joints" is debated, but it is generally attributed to two factors:

  1. Progesterone Receptor Antagonism: Stanozolol has been shown to act as an antagonist at the progesterone receptor. Progesterone and estrogen are critical for maintaining synovial fluid and collagen synthesis in the joints.
  2. Osmotic Shifts: Winstrol aggressively pulls water out of the extracellular spaces. This systemic drying effect reduces the fluid cushion inside the joint capsules, leading to friction, pain, and an increased risk of tendon rupture during heavy loads.[8]
11 · Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ).

confidence_tier: community

Q: Is injectable Winstrol safer for the liver than oral Winstrol? A: No. This is a common myth. Because the injectable version is still 17-alpha-alkylated, it must still be processed by the liver. The hepatotoxicity is virtually identical whether you swallow it or inject it.

Q: Can women use Winstrol? A: While some advanced female competitors use it, it is generally not recommended. The risk of virilization (deep voice, facial hair) is much higher with Winstrol than with Anavar or Primobolan.

Q: Why do sprinters use it if it hurts the joints? A: Sprinters use it for explosive power and central nervous system stimulation without adding body weight. The joint pain is usually manageable for track athletes compared to powerlifters who are moving massive external loads.
