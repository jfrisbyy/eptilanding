01 · Quick Overview

Quick Overview.

Anavar (chemical name: Oxandrolone) is an oral anabolic-androgenic steroid (AAS) derived from Dihydrotestosterone (DHT). It is widely considered one of the mildest, safest, and most well-tolerated oral steroids ever created. Originally developed in 1962 to treat muscle wasting diseases, it is still FDA-approved and actively used in clinical settings today, particularly for severe burn victims and pediatric growth disorders.[1]

In the fitness and bodybuilding community, Anavar is famous for producing clean, dry strength gains without water retention. Because of its extremely low androgenic rating, it is the most popular steroid used by female athletes (often called the "Girl Steroid"). While it is mild compared to other orals, it is still liver-toxic and will suppress natural testosterone production in men.[2]

  • Primary Use Case: Cutting, preserving muscle in a caloric deficit, increasing strength, and female enhancement.
  • Mechanism: Agonism of the androgen receptor, with a unique ability to significantly increase phosphocreatine synthesis in muscle cells.[3]
  • Who it is for: Beginners looking for a mild introduction to orals, female athletes, and advanced bodybuilders in the final weeks of contest prep.
  • Who it is NOT for: Men looking to add massive amounts of bulk or overall body weight.

02 · The Protocol & Usage Guide

The Protocol & Usage Guide.

confidence_tier: well-established

Anavar is a 17-alpha-alkylated (17aa) oral steroid, meaning its molecular structure has been altered to survive the first pass through the liver. It has a relatively short half-life of approximately 9 to 10 hours, requiring multiple doses per day to maintain stable blood levels.[4]

Standard Dosing Schedule

PhaseDoseFrequencyTiming
Men (Beginner/Cutting)30 mg to 50 mg / daySplit into 2 dosesAM and Pre-Workout
Men (Advanced)60 mg to 100 mg / daySplit into 2 dosesAM and Pre-Workout
Women (Beginner)5 mg to 10 mg / daySplit into 2 dosesAM and PM
Women (Advanced)15 mg to 20 mg / daySplit into 2 dosesAM and PM

Cycle Length & Discontinuation Protocol

  • Cycle Length (Men): 6 to 8 weeks. Despite being mild, it is still liver-toxic and should not be run indefinitely.
  • Cycle Length (Women): 4 to 6 weeks.
  • The "Test Base" Rule (Men): Anavar will suppress natural testosterone production. Men should run it alongside a Testosterone base.
  • Discontinuation (PCT): Men require a standard PCT (Nolvadex/Clomid) if not cruising on TRT. Women do not require a traditional PCT but should taper off the drug to allow hormones to normalize.

06 · Body Composition & Training Guide

Body Composition & Training Guide.

confidence_tier: well-established

  • Strength Gains: Anavar is famous for producing disproportionate strength gains relative to the amount of body weight gained. This makes it a favorite among powerlifters and fighters who need to stay in a specific weight class.
  • Fat Loss: While steroids do not burn fat directly like stimulants do, Anavar has been shown in clinical trials to significantly reduce visceral abdominal fat, likely due to its strong binding affinity to androgen receptors in adipose tissue.[5]
  • The "Pump": Users frequently report skin-tearing muscle pumps in the gym. While aesthetically pleasing, these pumps can become so severe in the lower back that they hinder exercises like deadlifts and squats.
07 · Storage, Handling & Accessibility

Storage, Handling & Accessibility.

confidence_tier: well-established

  • Storage: Store tablets at room temperature in a dry place.
  • WADA Status: Banned in competitive sports under section S1.1 (Anabolic Androgenic Steroids). Detection time is roughly 3 to 4 weeks.
  • Cost & Accessibility: Anavar is notoriously expensive to synthesize. Because of this, it is the most frequently faked steroid on the black market. Underground labs often substitute cheap Winstrol or Dianabol and label it as Anavar. Pharmaceutical-grade Anavar (Oxandrin) is available by prescription but is extremely costly.

10 · Deep Dive (For Advanced Researchers)

Deep Dive (For Advanced Researchers).

confidence_tier: well-established

The 17-Alpha-Alkylation and Hepatic Metabolism

Like most oral steroids, Oxandrolone is 17-alpha-alkylated, meaning a methyl group has been added at the 17th carbon position to prevent the liver from destroying the hormone before it reaches the bloodstream. However, Oxandrolone is unique among 17aa steroids. Clinical pharmacokinetic studies show that unlike Dianabol or Winstrol, which are heavily metabolized by the liver, approximately 30% of Oxandrolone is excreted in the urine completely unchanged. This indicates that it places significantly less metabolic burden on the liver, explaining why it is the only oral steroid routinely prescribed to patients with compromised immune systems or severe burns.[6]

Clinical Application in Burn Victims

Oxandrolone is the gold standard anabolic agent in modern burn units. Severe burns induce a massive hypermetabolic and catabolic state, where the body rapidly breaks down its own muscle tissue to fuel the healing process.

  • In randomized controlled trials, pediatric burn victims given Oxandrolone (0.1 mg/kg twice daily) showed significantly improved net protein balance, faster restoration of lean body mass, and shorter hospital stays compared to placebo.
  • Remarkably, studies have shown that the anabolic benefits of Oxandrolone in burn victims persist for up to 6 months after the drug is discontinued.[7][8]

Impact on Visceral Fat

While all androgens can reduce fat mass, Oxandrolone has a uniquely profound effect on visceral adipose tissue (deep belly fat). In a landmark study on older men, Oxandrolone administration resulted in a highly significant reduction in visceral fat compared to testosterone enanthate, despite both groups gaining similar amounts of lean mass. This is theorized to be due to Oxandrolone's specific binding affinity and its resistance to aromatization.[9]

11 · Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ).

confidence_tier: community

Q: Can I run an "Anavar-only" cycle? A: For women, yes. For men, it is highly discouraged. Anavar will suppress your natural testosterone production. Without a testosterone base, your estrogen will also crash, leading to lethargy, joint pain, and erectile dysfunction.

Q: How do I know if my Anavar is real? A: Because it is so expensive, Anavar is frequently faked. The only way to know for sure is via mass spectrometry testing (e.g., Janoshik). If you buy cheap Anavar, it is almost certainly Winstrol.

Q: Will Anavar make women look "manly"? A: At appropriate doses (5-10mg/day), the risk of virilization (deep voice, facial hair) is extremely low. However, if a woman pushes the dose to 20mg+ or runs it for many months, virilization can and will occur.
